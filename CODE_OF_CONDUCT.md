# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our standard / 我们的标准

We welcome technically rigorous and respectful collaboration across maritime operations, mobile systems, digital twins, reinforcement learning, control theory, safety engineering, and governance. Participants should distinguish measured, public, replayed, derived, and simulated data; disclose limitations and relevant interests; protect credentials and non-public information; and make reviews specific and reproducible.

我们欢迎围绕港航运营、移动系统、数字孪生、强化学习、控制理论、安全工程和治理开展严谨且相互尊重的协作。参与者应明确区分实测、公开、回放、派生和仿真数据，主动披露局限与相关利益，保护凭证与非公开信息，并使评审意见具体、可复现。

## Unacceptable conduct / 不可接受行为

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexualized attention, credential disclosure, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, deliberate unsafe guidance, and knowingly presenting sandbox or replay results as measured production evidence are unacceptable. Attempts to bypass human approval, authentication, safety interlocks, or fail-closed controls in a real operational setting are also outside the project scope.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、跟踪、人肉搜索、性暗示、泄露凭证、抄袭、伪造证据、故意提供不安全指导，以及明知是沙箱或回放结果却宣称为生产实测证据，均不可接受。任何试图在真实环境中绕过人工确认、身份认证、安全联锁或失败关闭控制的行为也不属于本项目允许范围。

## Scope and enforcement / 适用与执行

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, project events, and public representation of the project. Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, pause discussions, restrict participation, or refer severe matters to the relevant platform. Decisions should be proportionate and minimize unnecessary disclosure.

本准则适用于仓库讨论、代码评审、项目活动以及以项目名义进行的公开表达。维护者可按风险程度移除内容、关闭贡献、暂停讨论、限制参与，严重情况可移交平台处理，并应避免不必要地披露当事人信息。

Report security-sensitive matters privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Good-faith security research is welcome when it minimizes access and impact, avoids persistence or exfiltration, and gives maintainers a reasonable remediation window.
